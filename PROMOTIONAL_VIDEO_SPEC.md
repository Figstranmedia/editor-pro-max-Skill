## 🎨 DESIGN SPECIFICATIONS

### Color Palette (Brand Consistent)
- **Primary:** #6366f1 (Indigo - tech)
- **Secondary:** #0f0f23 (Dark blue - professional)
- **Accent:** #10b981 (Emerald - success/AI)
- **Text:** #ffffff (white on dark), #0f0f23 (dark on light)
- **Background:** Gradient 0f0f23 → 1a1a3e

### Typography
- **Headlines:** Inter Bold, 72-96pt (TikTok), 56-80pt (YouTube), 48-64pt (LinkedIn)
- **Body:** Inter Regular, 36-56pt
- **Mono (code):** JetBrains Mono, 24-32pt

### Transitions (per VIDEO_EDITING_SPECS.md)
- **Hook:** Zoom in (0.4s)
- **Problem → Solution:** Wipe right (0.6s)
- **Solution → Features:** Fade (0.5s)
- **Features → Stats:** Cut (0s, fast momentum)
- **Stats → CTA:** Dissolve (0.8s, cinematic)
- **Between scenes:** Mix of cuts and 0.3-0.5s transitions

---

## 🔊 AUDIO SPECIFICATIONS

### Music
- **Track:** Uplifting, tech, modern (Epidmic Sounds, Artlist, YouTube Audio Library)
- **BPM:** 120-130 (energetic but not frantic)
- **Duration:** 90s full track
- **Volume:** -12dB during voiceover, -6dB during silent scenes

### Voiceover (optional, for YouTube/LinkedIn)
- **Speaker:** Clear, confident, friendly
- **Speed:** 110-120 WPM
- **Tone:** Enthusiastic but professional
- **Recording:** 48kHz, noise-free

### Sound Effects
- Tech sounds: Subtle beeps, keyboard taps (low volume, -15dB)
- Success fanfare: Uplifting chord (0.3s, -10dB)
- Notifications: Platform-specific sounds (0.2-0.3s each, -12dB)
- Transitions: Whoosh, slide sounds (optional, -14dB)

## ✅ PRE-RENDER CHECKLIST

- ⬜ Hook captures attention in first 2s
- ⬜ Text legible on mobile (48pt+ for TikTok)
- ⬜ Pacing matches platform (fast for TikTok, medium for YouTube, slow for LinkedIn)
- ⬜ Transitions smooth and purposeful
- ⬜ Brand colors consistent (#6366f1, #0f0f23, #10b981)
- ⬜ Audio: VO clear, music supporting (not competing)
- ⬜ CTA explicit (GitHub link, website, "try now")
- ⬜ Aspect ratios correct (1080x1920 TikTok, 1920x1080 YouTube, 1200x628 LinkedIn)
- ⬜ No dead air or silence
- ⬜ Ending strong (last 5s most impactful)
- ⬜ Logo/branding present
- ⬜ Call-to-action clear and visible
