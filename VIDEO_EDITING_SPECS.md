# 🎬 Video Editing Specifications & Rules

Official editing rules for Editor Pro Max. These constraints improve clarity, reduce revisions, and ensure consistent professional output across all content types.

---

## 1️⃣ THE HOOK (Primeros 3 segundos)

**Goal:** Stop scroll, capture attention, establish context.

### Rule Set: Hook Elements

| Element | Duration | Rule | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Visual Hook** | 0-1s | Immediate contrast, movement, or text | Cut, zoom, flash, particle effect |
| **Text Hook** | 1-2s | Question, stat, or curiosity gap | "Did you know?", "Wait for it...", "You won't believe" |
| **Audio Hook** | 0-1s | Trending sound, beat drop, voice spike | TikTok trending, podcast clip, DJ drop |
| **Transition** | 0.3-0.5s | Fast snap, dynamic cut | Jump cut, slide, spin |

### Platform-Specific Hooks

**TikTok/Instagram Reels:**
- ✅ Zoom in on face (0.3s)
- ✅ Text overlay (bold, 72pt+)
- ✅ Trending audio match
- ❌ Slow fade-in (loses attention)
- ❌ Intro logo (kills engagement)

**YouTube/LinkedIn:**
- ✅ Statement or question
- ✅ Problem statement
- ✅ Value promise
- ✅ Slower, more professional
- ❌ Clickbait (damages trust)

**Twitter/Short Form:**
- ✅ Bold stat or quote
- ✅ Fast visual cut
- ✅ 2-3s max hook
- ❌ Generic open

## 2️⃣ PACING & RHYTHM

**Goal:** Match content rhythm to platform, maintain momentum, optimize retention.

### Pacing Rules by Duration

| Video Length | Avg Shot Duration | Cut Frequency | Mood |
|---|---|---|---|
| **3-5s** | 0.8-1.5s | Every 0.8s-1.5s | Fast, snappy |
| **6-15s** | 1.5-2.5s | Every 1.5-2.5s | Energetic |
| **15-30s** | 2-3s | Every 2-3s | Balanced |
| **30-60s** | 3-4s | Every 3-4s | Builds momentum |
| **1-3 min** | 4-6s | Every 4-6s | Narrative flow |
| **3+ min** | 6-10s | Every 6-10s | Conversational |

### Rhythm Patterns

**Fast Cuts** (TikTok/Reels trend):
- 0.5-1s per shot
- Match trending audio BPM
- Jump cuts create energy
- Best for: Entertainment, trends, comedy

**Medium Cuts** (Instagram/YouTube):
- 1.5-2s per shot
- Natural transitions
- Supports voiceover
- Best for: Tutorials, stories, lifestyle

**Slow Pacing** (LinkedIn/Educational):
- 3-6s per shot
- Text appears and stays
- Professional spacing
- Best for: B2B, education, deep-dives

## 3️⃣ TRANSITION SPEEDS & TYPES

**Goal:** Transition style must match content intent and platform norm.

### Transition Speed Rules

| Transition Type | Duration | Use Case | Platforms |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cut** | 0s | Energy, comedic timing | All |
| **Jump Cut** | 0s | Remove silence, quick pacing | TikTok, Reels, YouTube Shorts |
| **Crossfade** | 0.3-0.5s | Smooth shift, professional | All |
| **Slide** | 0.3-0.8s | Direction-aware flow | Reels, YouTube |
| **Wipe** | 0.5-1s | Dynamic, playful | Reels, TikTok |
| **Dissolve** | 0.5-1s | Soft transition, cinematic | LinkedIn, YouTube |
| **Zoom** | 0.4-0.8s | Emphasis, energy | TikTok, Reels |
| **Blur** | 0.3-0.5s | Focus shift | All |
| **Fade to Black** | 0.8-1s | Scene separation, emphasis | YouTube, Presentations |

### Forbidden Transitions

❌ Transitions > 1.5s (except fade to black for emphasis)
❌ Multiple transitions in sequence (max 2 back-to-back)
❌ Transition style mismatch (e.g., slow dissolve in fast TikTok)
❌ Overly complex transitions (>2 effects layered)

## 4️⃣ TEXT ON SCREEN

**Goal:** Legible, impactful, matches platform and device.

### Text Duration Rules

| Text Type | Min Duration | Recommended | Max Duration | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Headline** | 1.5s | 2-3s | 4s | Bold, anchor text |
| **Body Text** | 2s | 2.5-3s | 5s | Support headline |
| **Caption** | 2s | 2-2.5s | 3s | Subtitle, voiceover support |
| **CTA** | 2s | 2-3s | 5s | Call-to-action |
| **Stat/Number** | 2s | 2.5s | 4s | Bold emphasis |

### Font Size by Platform & Device

**TikTok/Instagram Reels (vertical 1080x1920):**
```
Headline:  72-96pt (mobile viewing ~6-8" screen)
Body:      48-64pt
Caption:   36-48pt
CTA:       64-80pt (must be large)
Min readable: 32pt
```

**YouTube (horizontal 1920x1080):**
```
Headline:  64-80pt (viewing 24-30" distance)
Body:      48-56pt
Caption:   36-44pt
CTA:       56-72pt
Min readable: 28pt
```

**LinkedIn (1200x628):**
```
Headline:  56-64pt
Body:      40-48pt
Caption:   32-40pt
CTA:       48-56pt
Min readable: 24pt
```

### Text Spacing & Readability

- ✅ Text centered or left-aligned (never right)
- ✅ Contrast ratio ≥ 4.5:1 (WCAG AA)
- ✅ Line height 1.4-1.6x font size
- ✅ Padding/margin 20-40px from edges
- ✅ Max 2 colors per text element
- ❌ Text <24pt on mobile
- ❌ Text over busy backgrounds (use semi-transparent bg)
- ❌ More than 3 text elements per frame

## 5️⃣ STYLE FIDELITY & BRAND CONSISTENCY

**Goal:** Every video reinforces brand identity, not confuse it.

### Brand Element Checklist

Before rendering, verify:

- ✅ **Colors:** Use brand palette (max 3 colors + white/black)
- ✅ **Fonts:** Max 2 font families (heading + body)
- ✅ **Logo:** Present (if brand video), consistent placement
- ✅ **Tone:** Matches brand voice (casual, formal, playful, etc)
- ✅ **Aspect ratio:** Correct for platform
- ✅ **Speed:** Matches brand energy level
- ✅ **Music:** Genre matches brand (not mismatched)

### Brand Consistency Rules

| Brand Type | Speed | Colors | Tone | Example |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Tech/Professional** | Medium-Fast | Cool tones (blues, grays) | Direct, confident | SaaS, B2B |
| **Lifestyle/Creator** | Fast | Warm, vibrant | Casual, relatable | Influencer, lifestyle |
| **Educational** | Medium | Varied | Clear, approachable | Online course |
| **Entertainment** | Very Fast | Bold, contrasting | Playful, humorous | Comedy, gaming |
| **Luxury** | Slow | Minimal, elegant | Sophisticated | High-end brand |

### Forbidden Style Breaks

❌ Sudden tone shift (serious to comedy without context)
❌ Color palette change mid-video
❌ Font mismatch (serif in sans-serif brand)
❌ Music genre mismatch (metal in corporate video)
❌ Logo/branding missing (except very intentional)

## 6️⃣ ENGAGEMENT ELEMENTS BY CONTENT TYPE

**Goal:** Maximize completion rate, shares, comments using proven patterns.

### Tutorial Content

**Hook:** Problem statement (2s)
**Pacing:** Medium (2-3s shots)
**Transitions:** Subtle (crossfade, slide)
**Text:** Step numbers, emphasis text (2-3s each)
**CTA:** "DM for more tips" or link
**Music:** Minimal, focus on voiceover
**Engagement Stats:** 45-60% average completion rate
**Best Time:** Posts mid-morning or evening

### Testimonial/Review

**Hook:** Quote or strong statement (2-3s)
**Pacing:** Slow-Medium (3-4s shots)
**Transitions:** Dissolve, fade
**Text:** Quote, name, title (2-5s)
**CTA:** "Try it" or link to product
**Visuals:** Face-forward, product shots
**Engagement Stats:** 60-75% completion (high trust)
**Best Time:** Business hours for B2B, evening for B2C

### Trend/Meme Content

**Hook:** Immediate (0.3-0.8s)
**Pacing:** Very fast (0.8-1.5s cuts)
**Transitions:** Cuts, quick dissolves
**Text:** Big, bold, minimal
**Music:** Trending audio REQUIRED
**Engagement Stats:** 25-35% completion (scroll-heavy)
**Best Time:** When trend is hot (hours matter)

### Educational/Explainer

**Hook:** Question or problem (2-3s)
**Pacing:** Medium (2-3s per concept)
**Transitions:** Slide, wipe (directional)
**Text:** Key concepts, definitions (3-4s)
**CTA:** "Subscribe for more" or course link
**Music:** Supportive, not distracting
**Engagement Stats:** 50-70% completion (dependent on topic)
**Best Time:** Evening/weekends

### Product Launch/Announcement

**Hook:** The product (1-2s) or benefit (2-3s)
**Pacing:** Fast initially, then medium
**Transitions:** Dynamic (zoom, cut)
**Text:** Product name, key features (3-4s each)
**CTA:** "Get it now" + link
**Music:** Uplifting, memorable
**Engagement Stats:** 40-55% completion
**Best Time:** Morning announcement → evening engagement

## 7️⃣ AUDIO GUIDELINES

**Goal:** Audio supports (not competes with) visuals. Clarity > loudness.

### Audio Layers

| Layer | Volume | Purpose | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Voiceover** | -6dB to -3dB | Information, narration | Clear, noise-free |
| **Music** | -15dB to -12dB (during VO) | Emotional backdrop | Royalty-free, on-brand |
| **SFX** | -12dB to -9dB | Emphasis, transitions | Subtle, not jarring |
| **Ambient** | -20dB to -18dB | Scene setting | Minimal |

### Music Strategy by Platform

**TikTok/Reels:** MUST use trending audio (0-2 days old)
**YouTube:** Original score or established tracks (longer shelf-life)
**LinkedIn:** Subtle background, VO-focused
**Twitter:** Minimal audio (people watch muted)
**Facebook:** Music + captions (sound often off)

### VO Rules

- ✅ Speak clearly at 110-120 WPM
- ✅ Leave 0.5s pauses between sentences
- ✅ Use professional mic (no phone audio)
- ✅ Normalize audio to -3dB
- ❌ Background noise, echo, clipping
- ❌ Monotone delivery (vary tone)
- ❌ Music louder than VO

## 8️⃣ CALL-TO-ACTION (CTA)

**Goal:** Clear next step. No ambiguity.

### CTA Placement & Timing

| Platform | Placement | Timing | Text |
|---|---|---|---|
| **TikTok** | Bottom 15-30% | Last 1-2s | "Follow for more" |
| **Instagram** | Overlay or caption | Last 2s | "Link in bio" |
| **YouTube** | 5-10s before end | Last 5-10s | Subscribe button |
| **LinkedIn** | End screen | Last 3s | "Comment your thoughts" |
| **Twitter** | Text or caption | Implicit | Link in bio |

### CTA Text Rules

✅ **Clear:** "Buy now" not "Check this out"
✅ **Action-oriented:** Verb-first ("Subscribe", "Join", "Learn")
✅ **Urgent:** "Limited time" or specific deadline
✅ **Benefit-focused:** "Get free template" not "Download"
✅ **Visible:** 48pt+ text, high contrast

## 9️⃣ RETENTION CURVE & EDITING STRATEGY

**Goal:** Flatten the retention drop. Edit to retain viewers through the middle.

### Typical Retention Curve (without editing)

```
100% ─────┐
        ╲
 75% ──────╲────
        ╲╲╲
 50% ──────╲────
           ╲
 25% ────────╲──
             ╲
  0% ─────────┴─ Video End
  0s    15s   30s   45s   60s
```

### Editing to Flatten Retention

**At 25% mark (potential drop):**
- Add text: "This is the important part"
- Add visual: Cut, zoom, new scene
- Add music: Beat drop, transition
- Remove: Silence, filler, slow pacing

**At 50% mark (major drop):**
- Introduce: New person, concept, or visual
- Shift: Pace up, surprise element
- Clarify: "Here's the summary..." or "Wait for it"

**At 75% mark (end drop):**
- CTA: "Subscribe for part 2"
- Teaser: "Next video..."
- Retention: Last 10s should be strongest

## 1️⃣1️⃣ REELS / TIKTOK / SHORTS — TEXT & SAFE ZONE RULES (2026)

> These rules are derived from current platform specs and top-performing creator patterns. Apply them to all vertical video (1080×1920).

### Safe Zones — Exact Pixels (1080×1920)

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────┐ ← 0px
│  TOP BLOCKED: 250px                     │   (account name, follow button, status bar)
├─────────────────────────────────────────┤ ← 250px  ← SAFE CONTENT STARTS
│                                         │
│   SAFE AREA: 1080 × 1320px             │
│   (centered)                            │
│                                         │
│   Right edge –120px: action buttons    │
│   (Like, Comment, Share, ⋯)            │
│                                         │
├─────────────────────────────────────────┤ ← 1570px ← SAFE CONTENT ENDS
│  BOTTOM BLOCKED: 350px (Instagram)     │   (captions bar, audio name, like/share icons)
│                  400px (TikTok)        │
└─────────────────────────────────────────┤ ← 1920px
```

**Implementation in editor-pro-max:**
- `CaptionOverlay` auto-detects vertical video and applies `bottom: 420px` safe offset
- Never place text below `y=1520` or above `y=280` in a 1920px canvas
- Right-side elements (watermark, CTAs) must be at least `130px` from the right edge

---

### Caption / Subtitle Rules

| Property | Rule | Value |
|---|---|---|
| **Font size** | Readable at arm's length on 6" phone | 55–75pt (default: 68pt) |
| **Font weight** | Always heavy | 800–900 |
| **Font family** | Sans-serif only | Inter, Montserrat, Bebas Neue |
| **Outline** | Mandatory — guarantees readability on any background | 3px black stroke |
| **Lines per page** | Max 2 lines | Break at ~7 words |
| **Chunk duration** | Word-by-word or 2–4 word groups | 600–900ms per chunk |
| **Position** | Lower-middle third | `bottom: 420px` from base |
| **Max width** | Never full width | 80–85% of frame |
| **Highlight color** | Active word highlight | Yellow `#FFEE00` or green `#39E508` |
| **Text color** | Base text | White `#ffffff` |

**Forbidden:**
- ❌ Thin fonts (< weight 600)
- ❌ Script/decorative fonts for captions
- ❌ Captions below y=1520 (hidden by platform UI)
- ❌ More than 2 lines visible at once
- ❌ Text right-aligned
- ❌ Low-contrast text (no outline, light text on light bg)

---

### Hook Text Rules (First 2.5s)

The hook is the most important element. 2026 standard:

| Property | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Position** | **Dead center** of frame — not top, not bottom |
| **Font size** | 80–120pt (large, dominant) |
| **Duration** | 2–2.5s visible (60–75 frames @ 30fps) |
| **Animation** | Scale-in (0.3s) → hold → fade (0.3s) |
| **Style** | Heavy weight + black outline (same as karaoke preset) |
| **Max words** | 5–8 words max |
| **Color** | White with thick black outline |

**Patterns that perform:**
- Question: "Did you know this?" → center, 96pt
- Stat: "90% of people fail here." → center, 88pt
- Curiosity gap: "Wait until the end." → center, 96pt
- Bold claim: "This changed everything." → center, 88pt
